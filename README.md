On The Way

Peer-to-peer ridesharing for Sri Lanka

The problem

Sri Lanka's roads are full of private cars carrying mostly empty seats, while the buses beside them are packed past comfort. With diesel at record prices — 458 LKR per litre — drivers carry the full cost of every trip alone, and commuters carry the discomfort. Both sides lose, every single day.

Our answer

On The Way connects drivers who have empty seats with passengers heading the same direction. It isn't a taxi service — drivers are hosts sharing a route they were already taking, and the fuel cost is split fairly and automatically. Drivers recover their costs; passengers ride in comfort for a fraction of a taxi fare.

How it works

1

Set your route

Enter your start and end points on the map.

2

Get matched

We find drivers and passengers already travelling your way.

3

Share the ride

Meet up, ride together, and split the cost automatically.

For passengers

Up to 60% cheaper than taxis or tuk-tuks for a daily commute.

A guaranteed seat in a private car instead of a crowded bus.

Pickups along the route — you join the driver's existing journey.

For drivers

Recover fuel costs on trips you were making anyway.

Not a taxi job — you're a host sharing your route with neighbours and colleagues.

Automatic fair pricing, calculated from distance. No haggling.

Built-in safety, not bolted on

Verified users

Drivers are vetted with licence, insurance, and vehicle photo checks before their first ride.

Live trip sharing

Share your trip location with family or friends in real time, for every ride.

Circles of trust

Ride within networks you already belong to — office colleagues, campuses, alumni groups.

Mutual ratings

Drivers and passengers rate each other after every trip, both ways.
